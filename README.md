# The Collector - AFK

**Version**: v.1.2.3

## 📖 Description
**The Collector - AFK** is an addictive idle/clicker game where your goal is to amass wealth, collect rare artifacts, and explore the depths of mysterious worlds. Start by mining simple crystals, then automate your empire with advanced technology and magical tools.

Even when you are not playing, your production continues!

## ✨ Features
- **💎 Idle Mining System**: Click to mine or build an army of auto-miners (Drones, Farms, and more).
- **🌍 Explore Zones**: Travel through the **Crystal Cave**, **Magma Chamber**, **Void Expanse**, and the **Ancient Sanctum**. Each zone offers unique multipliers.
- **⚔️ Equipment & Rarity**: Loot usage items like Drills and Batteries, or find powerful Armor and Relics. Rarities range from **Common** to **Legendary**.
- **🛒 Dynamic Shop**: Check back daily for new deals on supplies and limited-edition gear.
- **👻 Ascension System**: Reset your progress to collect **Souls** and gain permanent power.
- **💤 Offline Progress**: Your miners keep working at 50% efficiency while you are away.

## 🕹️ How to Play
1. **Start Mining**: Click the main crystal to generate your first resources.
2. **Upgrade**: Use crystals to buy **Click Power** upgrades and **Auto-Clickers**.
3. **Equip Gear**: Open your inventory and equip items to boost your stats.
4. **Travel**: Unlock new zones to multiply your crystal generation.
5. **Ascend**: When progress slows down, use the **Prestige** feature to restart with powerful Souls.

## 🛠️ Installation
Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser to start playing! No installation required.
